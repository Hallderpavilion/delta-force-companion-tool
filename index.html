Why you need a companion

Delta Force lacks in-depth match analytics. You can't track your stats, optimize loadouts, or learn map intel without third-party tools. This companion fixes that.

Features

Match Analytics

Track K/D, win rate, favorite weapons, and playstyle trends.

Loadout Planner

Build, save, and share optimal loadouts for every operator.

Weapon Stats

Compare damage, recoil, TTK, and range across all weapons.

Map Intel

Interactive maps with callouts, spawn points, and objective locations.

Performance Tracker

Monitor FPS, ping, and system stats in real time.
